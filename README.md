## About Me

I'm a 7th-semester Computer Engineering student at Pillai College of Engineering (Mumbai University), specializing in Cyber Security. My work sits at the intersection of offensive security, full-stack development, and applied AI/ML — I like understanding systems well enough to both build and break them.

Most of my projects start from a practical itch: **Sentinel** grew out of wanting a hands-on way to teach red team/blue team concepts instead of just reading about them, and **ZeroKey Auth** came from digging into why passwordless auth is genuinely harder to get right than it looks. Alongside project work, I've competed in live hackathons (Adobe University Hackathon 2026) and CTF challenges (HackTheBox), and I regularly work through VAPT methodology, network security, and penetration testing coursework.

I'm currently looking for opportunities in cybersecurity, security engineering, or backend/full-stack roles where I can keep building things that need to hold up under real scrutiny.
